Escalate to a human

When evidence is conflicting, unavailable, outdated, jurisdiction-sensitive or outside scope, the system should abstain and offer a human IP facilitator route.

Production security should include privacy, auditability and access controls aligned to applicable data-protection requirements. Paid databases should only be connected with explicit user permission and logged access.
