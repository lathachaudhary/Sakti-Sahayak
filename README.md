IP-SAKTI Sahayak

AI-Assisted Intellectual Property & Regulatory Guidance Platform for AYUSH

IP-SAKTI Sahayak is a prototype platform designed to help AYUSH practitioners, researchers, startups, MSMEs, cultivators and innovators navigate intellectual property (IP), traditional knowledge (TK), Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS), and product-regulatory pathways.

The prototype focuses on classifying an AYUSH product first, routing it to relevant IP/protection areas, keeping Indian and international legal layers separate, and providing an evidence-oriented workflow with human escalation.

«Prototype: This project is for demonstration and educational purposes. It does not provide legal advice.»

---

🎯 Problem

Ayurvedic innovations may involve several overlapping areas, including:

- Patents
- Geographical Indications (GI)
- Trademarks
- Copyright
- Industrial Designs
- Trade Secrets
- Plant Variety Protection
- Traditional Knowledge (TK)
- Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS)
- AYUSH product classification and regulation
- Food, cosmetic and advertising requirements

Navigating these areas can be difficult because the applicable requirements depend on the product, formulation, intended use, source of knowledge/resources and jurisdiction.

---

💡 Proposed Solution

IP-SAKTI Sahayak provides a guided workflow that:

1. Collects basic product/formulation information.
2. Classifies the product pathway.
3. Identifies potentially relevant IP routes.
4. Screens for traditional-knowledge and biological-resource considerations.
5. Separates Indian and international legal layers.
6. Provides an evidence/source-oriented answer flow.
7. Highlights uncertainty and recommends human review where appropriate.
8. Supports multiple languages in the prototype.

---

🚀 Key Features

1. Guided Product Assessment

Users can enter information about:

- Product/formulation
- Formulation source
- Intended use
- Technical/scientific contribution
- Biological resources or associated TK
- Commercialisation

The prototype then generates a structured assessment.

2. IP Router

The platform represents major protection routes:

- Patent
- GI
- Trademark
- Copyright
- Design
- Trade Secret
- Plant Variety Protection
- TK / Prior Art

3. Traditional Knowledge & Prior Art

The workflow highlights the need to check whether an Ayurvedic formulation or claimed subject is already represented in codified or community-held traditional knowledge before treating it as novel.

4. ABS Navigator

The prototype screens basic facts related to:

- Biological resources
- Associated traditional knowledge
- Commercial purpose
- Source/community information

It identifies situations that may require further review.

5. Jurisdiction Separation

The platform separates:

India

from

International

legal and regulatory layers to reduce the risk of mixing different legal frameworks.

6. Ask Sahayak

Users can select a jurisdiction and topic and enter a focused question.

The prototype demonstrates an intended retrieval-and-evidence workflow.

7. Source Library

The prototype provides links to official information sources, including:

- IP India
- WIPO
- Ministry of AYUSH
- FSSAI
- India Code
- Convention on Biological Diversity

8. Audit & Human Escalation

The prototype includes:

- Confidence indication
- Evidence trail concept
- Activity log
- Human escalation
- Safe abstention concept

⚠️ Disclaimer

IP-SAKTI Sahayak is a prototype for information and research assistance.

It is not a substitute for legal, regulatory, patent or professional advice.

A production implementation should verify:

- Current legislation and rules
- Jurisdiction
- Effective dates
- Regulatory category
- Official authority
- Source version
- Relevant case law or registry records

Where information is uncertain, conflicting, outdated or outside the system's scope, the system should abstain and recommend human review.
